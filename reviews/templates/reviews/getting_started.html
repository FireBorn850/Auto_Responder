1

Connect your business

From your Dashboard, click Sync Reviews in the top action bar. Enter your business name exactly as it appears on Google, and optionally your Google Place ID for a precise match.

Once synced, your real reviews appear in the Customer Reviews Feed, and your Total Reviews, Avg Rating, and Response Rate stats populate automatically.

2

Configure AI & Tone Settings

Head to AI & Tone Settings to control how replies are generated:

  • Automation Mode — choose Smart Guardrail (auto-post 4-5★, hold 1-3★ for review), Hands-Free (auto-approve everything), or Copilot (manual approval on every reply).
  • Brand Voice — pick Warm & Friendly, Professional & Formal, or Casual & Modern, plus a signature sign-off.
  • Response Length & Creativity — control how long replies are and how much wording varies.
  • Blacklisted Words — words the AI should never use (competitor names, etc.) — checked and auto-rewritten if one slips through.
  • Custom Business Knowledge Base — tell the AI facts about your business (parking, signature dishes, policies) so replies never sound generic.

Use Preview with Sample Review to see exactly how your current settings would respond, in both English and French, before saving.

3

Understand the Dashboard

Your Dashboard is the daily control center:

  • Filter reviews by status (Pending, Approved, Posted, Flagged) or jump straight to Negative reviews.
  • Click Generate AI Response on any review without a draft yet.
  • Edit any AI draft directly in the text box before clicking Approve & Post.
  • Competitor Benchmarking lets you track your rating against nearby businesses.
  • Recurring Complaint Themes uses AI to cluster your 1-3★ reviews into patterns worth fixing.
4

Set up QR Code Booster

Generate a trackable QR code that links straight to your Google review box — print it on table tents, receipts, or stickers.

Add a Private Feedback URL (like a simple Google Form) to enable the Smart Rating Gate: customers are asked "How was your visit?" first — 4-5★ routes to your public Google review, 1-3★ routes privately to you instead, so upset customers reach you before the public review page.

5

Connect TripAdvisor & Webhooks

In Integrations, sync your TripAdvisor listing the same way as Google — reviews from both sources land side by side in your dashboard.

For custom sources (a booking system, POS, or your own form), use your unique Webhook Endpoint — POST a review as JSON and it gets the same AI drafting and automation as any synced review. You can test it directly from the Integrations page before going live.

6

Try the Review Simulator

Not ready to sync real reviews yet? Use the Review Simulator to inject test reviews and watch the full AI pipeline — spam filtering, sentiment analysis, drafting, and automation routing — happen live, without touching your real Google Business Profile.

Quick tips

  • Daily AI generation has a limit shown in the top bar (e.g. "9/50 AI today") — this resets at midnight.
  • If a review is Flagged, it likely failed an authenticity or spam check — you can still click "Generate anyway" to override it.
  • Run AI Training (in AI & Tone Settings) after you've manually edited a few drafts — it helps future replies start closer to your real voice.
  • Use the theme toggle (sun/moon icon) in the header to switch between dark and light mode — your preference is remembered.
