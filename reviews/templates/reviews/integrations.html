Connect Any Platform

Sync SwissReply.AI with your CRM, helpdesk, or property management system — Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Mews, Cloudbeds, or anything else that can send an HTTP request. Connect directly, or route through Zapier or Make for a no-code setup. POST a review as JSON to your unique, secret URL below and it's picked up for AI drafting like any synced review. Anyone with this URL can create reviews under this business — don't share it publicly.
